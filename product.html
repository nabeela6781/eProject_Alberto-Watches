01 — THE HEART OF THE WATCH

Precision Movement

The movement is the heart of a timepiece. It is the mechanism responsible for keeping time and coordinating the hands of the watch.

ALBERTO's design philosophy places importance on dependable timekeeping and practical performance. Depending on the selected model, a watch may use a quartz or mechanical movement, each offering its own distinctive experience.

This combination of engineering and elegant presentation makes the movement an essential part of the watch's identity.

02 — ADVANCED DETAILS

Technology That Matters

Every detail has a purpose. Explore the important technologies and design features that contribute to the character of a watch.

Watch Movement

The movement controls the watch's timekeeping functions. Quartz movements use an electronic oscillator, while mechanical movements operate through carefully arranged mechanical components.

Crystal Protection

The crystal covers and protects the watch dial. Sapphire crystal is valued in watchmaking for its excellent scratch resistance and clear appearance.

Durable Case

The watch case protects its internal components and gives the timepiece its visual character. Its finishing and construction contribute to durability and style.

Water Resistance

Water resistance depends on the seals and construction of each model. The stated rating should always be checked before exposing a watch to water.

Dial Visibility

Clear markers, balanced hands and thoughtful dial layouts help make the time easy to read while maintaining a refined appearance.

Strap Engineering

A carefully designed strap or bracelet supports a comfortable fit. Its material and finishing complete the overall look of the timepiece.
