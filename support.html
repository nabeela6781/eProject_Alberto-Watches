PRESERVE YOUR TIMEPIECE

The Art of Watch Care

A luxury timepiece deserves attention and care. Simple maintenance habits can help preserve its appearance and keep it ready for every occasion.

Keep It Clean

Use a soft, clean cloth to gently wipe your watch and remove everyday dust.

Store It Safely

Keep your timepiece in a clean, dry and secure place when not in use.

Avoid Extreme Conditions

Protect your watch from extreme temperatures, strong impacts and unnecessary exposure to moisture.

Follow Model Instructions

Always check the specific care and water-resistance guidance for your watch model.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about your timepiece.

Gently wipe your watch with a soft, clean cloth when needed. Always follow the cleaning instructions for your specific watch material and model.

Water resistance varies by watch model. Check the water-resistance rating and manufacturer's instructions before exposing your watch to water.

Store your watch in a clean, dry and secure place. Using a suitable watch box can help protect it from dust and accidental scratches.

If you notice unusual timekeeping, visible damage or problems with the watch's functions, refer to the product manual and seek appropriate service advice.

Your warranty details should be provided with your purchase documentation. Keep your warranty card and proof of purchase in a safe place.

Check the product documentation, model information or markings provided with your timepiece. These details can help identify your specific watch model.
